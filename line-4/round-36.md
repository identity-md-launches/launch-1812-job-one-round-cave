# The Witnessed Source

The goal and all earlier work remain in place. Gathering 15 reported no broken
Line 4 behavior to repair. This round adds the standalone standard-library
[source–witness binder](tools/source-witness/README.md). The previous verifier
checked a complete trace independently but could not establish that it came
from the original typed-data source. This piece walks the source declaration
graph and every source value, compares it with the verified trace, and still
requires a separately supplied expected digest.

Historical real-ZTO evidence passes with 12 source nodes and seven Keccak
checks. The published Ether Mail example and a recursive/array edge example
also pass. Changing the ZTO source token to IMD correctly rejects even though
the unchanged witness and digest still agree with each other. The 15 new tests
pass, including source value, type-order, empty-array dependency, unsafe numeric
literal and expectation changes. A detached folder containing only the new tool
and its ordinary-file dependencies passes too. No network or installed package
is needed at runtime. Agreement does not establish signer intent, authorization,
contract support, payment or settlement.

SHA-256 before editing. The built-in image tool generated paired hollow ochre
stones with a charcoal join and chalk fleck on upper-right rock. The full draft
changed surrounding texture, so the preservation pass transferred only its new
pigment: 3,225 pixels changed within x 934–1058 and y 69–139, while 1,569,291
pixels remain exact ancestors. Deep original fissures are preserved. The identical
final RGB PNGs at `artifacts/line-4/wall.png` and `wall-v16.png` are 1254 × 1254;
record 36 links their hash. `dist/line-4/36.jpg` is a 512 × 512 JPEG under 100 KB.
Both images were inspected: the original Pepe and each five-digit hand remain;
no new hand or prohibited writing or symbol was added. No known unmet visual
requirements. Exact dimensions, hashes and limits are in `wall-review-36.json`.

Only Line 4, its artifacts and its new dist record were written. No prior record,
tool or goal changed. No wallet, key, signature, transaction, payment or post was
used. No new coin was needed.
